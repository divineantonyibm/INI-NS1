02

Plan and Execute DNS Changes

Intent-to-execution DNS orchestration with multi-agent NOC

Core INI Fit
Survey tested concept: "Create a proposed plan for DNS changes, allow you to review and approve it, and then make the approved changes on your behalf."

Persona

Infrastructure Architect
Secondary: SecOps / NOC. Approves and audits production edits

Trigger

An overloaded EU data centre needs traffic moved to US East and APAC while regional data-compliance rules still hold.

Goal

Execute a multi-zone emergency migration, for example shedding 40% of EU traffic, without manual record edits.

Control mode

Human-in-the-loop approval

1Express intent
2Review draft plan
3Policy check
4Approve
5Execute
6Verify & audit
Does
Describes the migration goal in the INI Chat Assistant or links the incident ticket.
Reads the proposed changes, ordering and rollback script.
Sees results of change-policy and compliance checks.
Approves the plan (human-in-the-loop gate); SecOps co-signs if required.
INI applies the change through NS1 Connect.
Watches live telemetry; if errors rise, the agent pauses and asks for approval to roll back.

INI × NS1 support

Build Advanced Traffic Steering

Telemetry-guided Filter Chain & Pulsar policy synthesis

High INI Fit
Survey tested concept: "Help you design advanced traffic steering configurations by explaining available options and helping determine how traffic should be routed based on your intended outcome."

Persona

Infrastructure Architect
Secondary: DevOps Engineer. Designs multi-CDN and hybrid-cloud steering

Trigger

A new multi-CDN or multi-region rollout needs a steering policy that satisfies performance, cost and GDPR constraints.

Goal

Turn a business routing goal into a Filter Chain that balances latency, transit cost, data sovereignty and origin capacity.

Control mode

Interactive chat assistant

1Define outcome
2Explore options
3Review draft chain
4Check bounds
5Save as reusable design
6Apply & refine
Does
States the goal in plain language: lowest latency in-region, cap transit spend, keep EU data in the EU.
Asks which filters exist and how they interact.
Reads the recommended order, for example UP/DOWN, SHED_LOAD, GEODNS, PULSAR, SELECT_FIRST_N.
Reviews warnings about blackholing, circular fallbacks and single-region overload.
Accepts the design and saves the pattern for reuse (proposed).
Applies the config (via UC 4 and UC 2) and tunes using Pulsar results.
Touchpoints
INI Chat Assistant
Assistant chat, NS1 docs (RAG)
Filter Chain preview, Pulsar RUM feeds
Validation panel, reasoning trace
Saved designs library (proposed)
NS1 Connect, Pulsar

04

Validate Configurations Before Deployment

Pre-deployment DNS simulation & blast-radius verification

High INI Fit
Survey tested concept: "Review or simulate a proposed DNS configuration before it is deployed to identify potential problems and show how the configuration is expected to behave."

Persona

Network Engineer
Secondary: SecOps. Owns a flagship service and its change approvals

Trigger

A weight shift on legacy origins could overload a smaller secondary data centre if a TTL or chain logic bug slips through.

Goal

Prove a routing change is safe before it reaches production, with evidence a change board will accept.

Control mode

Pre-flight simulation (proposed)

1Prepare change
2Run simulation
3Review blast radius
4Inspect reasoning
5Fix & re-test
6Gate & deploy
Does
Drafts the new weights and Filter Chain in a branch or ticket.
Requests a replay of the previous 24 hours of query telemetry against the proposal.
Reads the predicted traffic heatmap and blast-radius score.
Opens the reasoning trace to see how the score was reached.
Adjusts weights or fallbacks and re-runs until under threshold.
Pipeline gate passes (under an agreed unhandled-traffic limit); CAB receives the report.
Touchpoints
NS1 Connect, change ticket
INI simulation, NS1 config evaluation (proposed)
Heatmap, risk report
Reasoning trace
Chat assistant, simulation
CI/CD gate, CAB report
Thinks
"I think this is right, but I cannot be sure."
"Show me what really happens."
"Which sites get hit, and by how much?"
"Can I defend this number to the CAB?"
"Quick loop, I can iterate safely."
"Nobody has to guess."
Feels
AnxiousNeutralAnxiousConfidentConfidentDelighted
Pain points
No safe way to test against real traffic; reviews rely on eyeballing config.
Waiting for results with no sense of how long or how reliable they are.
Raw numbers without context are hard to interpret.
Black-box scores are not trusted by approvers.
Slow iteration would push teams to skip validation.
Manual gate evidence is time consuming to assemble.
INI × NS1 support
Proposed config is submitted to INI for pre-flight review.
Predictive replay of recent query telemetry against the proposed config (proposed capability).
Risk report highlights overloaded origins and TTL collisions in plain language.
Explainable audit trail with assumptions and inputs.
INI suggests specific fixes; each re-run is fast and logged.
Auto-generated change evidence; the gate blocks deployment above the threshold.
