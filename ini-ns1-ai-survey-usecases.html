Technical Architecture

The Three Pillars of INI's AI Pipeline

IBM Network Intelligence is built on watsonx foundation models and distributed data processing. It bridges the gap between high-volume telemetry streams and structured function execution.

Pillar 1: Data Pipeline & Semantic Topology
Normalizes heterogeneous metric streams across IP routing, RAN, optical, and DNS edge points into standard tuples (Device, Component, Metric Indicator). Telemetry retains full topological context, allowing AI models to detect cascading failures across organizational boundaries.
Pillar 2: Tripartite NOC Agent System
Each anomaly is handled by three collaborative sub-agents:
  • Reasoning Agent: Evaluates anomalous patterns, generates root-cause hypotheses, and formulates remediation strategies.
  • RAG Agent: Searches vector-embedded Methods of Procedure (MOPs), vendor RFCs, and runbooks to ground plans in verified standards.
  • Function-Calling Agent: Translates verified plans into exact, typed API payloads for target endpoints (NS1 Connect, Cisco IOS, JunOS).
Pillar 3: Dual Operating Modes & Guardrails
Supports Assistant Mode (human operator approval gate with transparent chain-of-thought inspection) and Autonomous Mode (sub-second zero-touch execution for pre-certified low-risk operational patterns).

Phased Rollout Strategy

To maximize practitioner trust and ensure safe enterprise adoption, capability rollout should strictly follow three sequential phases:

Phase 1: Observation (UC 01)

Stream NS1 telemetry into INI for read-only anomaly detection and correlation. Zero risk of production disruption.

Phase 2: Intent-Based Design & Pre-Flight Safety (UC 03, 04)

Enable interactive traffic steering policy synthesis and simulated dry-run validation. Changes are proposed with full View reasoning audit trails for human review.

Phase 3: Agentic Execution & Closed-Loop Control (UC 02)

Deploy NOC multi-agent execution with human-in-the-loop approval gates. Transition certified, routine remediation runbooks to Autonomous Mode with sub-minute automated rollback.
